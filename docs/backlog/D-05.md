## Premise status

**net-new.** Nothing to reproduce in `core/`; stage 1 does not run. The blocking dependency is not schedule but **fidelity** — an "immutable log" exported from a journal that duplicates and mutates entries across restarts is a compliance artefact that lies. Three Core-side facts as of 2026-08-11:

- **A-07** — replay re-appends completed steps, so the journal grows by a full copy of them every restart, and a duplicated `step:completed` mutates the existing trace row in place (pack Traps; read, not executed).
- **V-b**, unfiled and **executed** during F-01.2 verification — after a resume the journal holds **two** `subworkflow:started` entries for one exec id with *different* `childWorkflowId` (`internal/actors/workflow_handler.go:917-926` appends unconditionally). Postgres does not catch it: `sub_workflow_refs` is UNIQUE on `child_workflow_id` only. The fix belongs with **A-02**.
- Approvals are not addressable yet (**C-07**) and have no typed payload (**C-05**); "memory writes" are **C-02**, net-new.

Tier A is not green: F-01 completed 2026-08-11 with all three findings REPRODUCED against real Go 1.26.
